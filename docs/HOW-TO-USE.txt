STUCK AT ANY POINT? ASK THE APP ITSELF
   Click the gold "🎬 Producer" button at the bottom-right of ANY tab and ask your
   question in plain words — "how do I put myself in a scene?", "where is the clip
   tool?", "why can't I find my video?". It knows every tab and button of this studio
   and answers with the exact steps. Use its 📖 Detailed / ⚡ Brief buttons to choose
   between full instructions and a quick summary.

FIRST TIME YOU RUN IT
   1. Double-click NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-portable.exe — ONCE. (Or use the "NIHILPOINTZERO-OS"
      shortcut on your Desktop — it points straight at this exe.)
   2. The first launch takes ~40-60 seconds with no window showing (it's unpacking).
      WAIT. Do not double-click again.
   3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info -> Run anyway (it's safe,
      just unsigned).

HOW TO KNOW YOU'RE ON THE NEWEST BUILD  (READ THIS — it caused a lot of confusion)
   • The sidebar shows a small gold badge under "OS", e.g.
        v0.1.1 · 2026-07-19 15:59 · 4e5e704
     That reads: version · the exact date+time the build was made · a code id.
     The badge is now written AUTOMATICALLY at build time (it can never be forgotten),
     and it IS your proof of which build is running. Older/missing badge = stale copy.
   • PORTABLE EXE ONLY: do not pin it to the taskbar/Start while it's open — Windows
     freezes that pin to a TEMPORARY unpacked copy and keeps launching OLD code forever.
     Launch it from the Desktop shortcut or the .exe in this folder.
   • INSTALLED VERSION (run NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe once): pinning is completely
     SAFE — it runs from a fixed location, so pins always launch the current code, and
     it opens in seconds. This is the recommended way to use the studio on your own PC;
     keep the portable exe for USB travel.

IF THE AI STOPS ANSWERING  (READ THIS — it was a real bug, now fixed)
   The free online AI this app shipped with STOPPED BEING FREE (their decision, not a
   fault on your PC). It now replies "payment required" or "model not found".
   • The app now falls back automatically to Ollama — the free AI that runs on YOUR PC.
     If answers still come, that's where they're coming from. Nothing to do.
   • Every AI failure is now written down. Settings -> "Known Issues" shows exactly what
     went wrong and when, with the service's own words. The full file is at
     nihilpointzero-data\logs\ai-errors.log
   • Settings -> "🩺 Run full check" now really tests the AI (it used to check the wrong
     address, which is why it stayed green during the whole outage).
   • For the best free setup: keep Ollama installed and running. No key, no internet
     needed, nothing to pay.

REAL AI VIDEO — WHAT'S NEW, AND WHAT'S STILL TRUE  (being straight with you)
   NEW: Video Studio now has "REAL AI video — free cloud" — real moving video per
   scene, not a photo slideshow. TWO free routes (pick in Settings -> AI Video):
   • Pollinations (recommended here): sign up at enter.pollinations.ai with GitHub
     or email — NO phone number — create a free key and paste the SECRET (sk_…) one
     into Settings. Free "Pollen" comes from that dashboard's QUESTS tab: claim the
     rewards you already qualify for (e.g. "Create your first API key" pays 0.25 —
     about five 5-second scenes). A "Test key" button in Settings shows your balance
     without spending anything.
   • Puter (Google's Veo): no key at all — a sign-in window pops up during the first
     build. Heads-up: Puter's verification rejects some countries' phone numbers
     (Pakistani numbers included) — if their sign-up won't finish, use Pollinations.
   The honest catches for both: the free allowances are small; only up to 5 scenes
   per build get real motion (the rest use AI stills — adjustable in Settings); and
   each real-motion scene takes minutes. When an allowance runs out, scenes fall
   back to AI still images and the build log says why. Nothing breaks.
   STILL TRUE: LOCAL AI "motion video" and "talking photo" models (LTX-Video, Wan 2.2,
   SadTalker, LivePortrait) need a DEDICATED NVIDIA graphics card. This laptop's
   graphics are built into the processor, so those cannot run here at all — not even
   slowly. The option is now visible but greyed out ("Requires NVIDIA GPU — not
   detected on this system"), and you can set it all up in Settings -> AI Video
   today — it unlocks by itself the day this PC has an NVIDIA card.
   Also free: photo slideshows from AI images (16 styles), real stock footage, your
   own footage and recordings, narrated in natural voices.

ON YOUR PHONE
   Two ways, and they're different:
   1) THE WHOLE STUDIO, ON YOUR PHONE - Settings -> "Phone access". Open the link it
      shows. You get the REAL studio: the same tabs, the same buttons, the same
      everything you see on the laptop. Not a smaller version - the actual app. Your PC

   2) THE PHONE APP - for when your PC is OFF. Open
      https://dskjazz.github.io/nihilpointzero-phone/ once, then browser menu ->
      "Install app". A gold circle icon lands on your home screen. Ideas, scripts,
      scenes, thumbnail briefs, the prompter and the Advisor, anywhere, on mobile data.
   Nothing on the phone can change or delete your work on the PC.

RECORDING SO IT DOESN'T LOOK LIKE A PHONE
   Recorder tab -> pick Camera, Screen, or "Voice only - no face".
   • The number that matters is the bitrate, and the Recorder now sets it to exactly
     what YouTube asks uploads to be (8 Mbps at 1080p30, 45 at 4K30). Browsers
     otherwise default to about 2.5 Mbps at ANY size, which is why phone recordings
     usually look soft. The Recorder shows you the figure and the size per minute.
   • Choose the frame rate too: 24 (cinematic), 30 (normal), 60 (smooth, best for
     screens and movement).
   • "Voice only" opens no camera at all - no permission prompt, no camera light. Your
     narration is saved as an audio track to put under scenes or stock footage.
   • Filming on the phone: in the phone app, "Film it now" opens your phone's OWN camera
     app rather than recording inside the page. That uses the real sensor, hardware
     encoding, stabilisation and autofocus, and is by a distance the best-looking
     option. Front camera for your face, back camera for everything else.
   • Long takes recorded on the phone are saved to the phone's Downloads rather than
     squeezed over the link. Short ones go straight to the PC.
