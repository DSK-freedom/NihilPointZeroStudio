1. WHAT THIS IS / FIRST LAUNCH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You type (or speak) an idea; the app writes a script, makes the visuals, adds a
voice, music and captions, and gives you a finished video — or you record your own
voice over it. Most of it is FREE and offline. The smart-AI bits use the internet
(also free — keys are built in).

TWO WAYS TO RUN IT:
  PORTABLE (the classic way): double-click NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-portable.exe ONCE and
  wait ~40-60s (no window shows while it unpacks — normal). This is the copy that
  travels on a USB with your whole folder.
  INSTALLED (recommended for daily use on THIS PC): run NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe
  once. From then on the app opens in ~2 seconds, taskbar pins are SAFE, and it uses
  the SAME nihilpointzero-data folder — your videos/settings appear in both versions.
If SmartScreen warns: More info -> Run anyway (it's safe, just unsigned). The
"NIHILPOINTZERO-OS" Desktop shortcut points at the portable exe.

WHICH BUILD AM I RUNNING? The sidebar shows a small gold badge under "OS", e.g.
"v0.1.1 · 2026-07-19 15:59 · 4e5e704" — that's version · the exact date+time the
build was made · a code id. It is stamped AUTOMATICALLY when the app is built, so it
can never be wrong or forgotten; it is proof of which code is running.
IMPORTANT (PORTABLE EXE ONLY): do NOT pin the portable app to the taskbar/Start
WHILE it's open — Windows freezes that pin to a temporary unpacked copy and it will
launch OLD code forever. Launch from the Desktop shortcut or the .exe in this folder.
The INSTALLED version has no such trap — pin it freely. (The pin trap once made hours
of updates look like "nothing changed".) See §13 if a build looks stale.

2. THE ONE RULE + MOVING TO ANOTHER PC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your work lives inside the "nihilpointzero-data" folder next to the .exe. ALWAYS
copy the WHOLE "NihilPointZeroStudio" folder — never just the .exe — or your work
won't be found.

Copy the folder to another PC and:
  TRAVELS AUTOMATICALLY: all videos, scripts, Script Pad, library, settings, the
     built-in free keys, and the Natural voice (if downloaded).
  NEEDS INTERNET (no install): AI writing/images/visuals, put-me-in-a-scene, online
     music removal.
  YOU REDO ONCE (optional): offline Demucs music separation — it's a Python program
     tied to a machine. On the new PC: install Python (python.org), then in a command
     window run  python -m pip install demucs numpy , then Settings -> "Music
     separation (local)" -> type  python -m demucs  -> Save. You do NOT need this if
     you have internet (the Online button already removes music).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Easiest: ✦ AI Command -> type/🎤 "Write a 1-minute script about the rupee and build
it in 1080p with calm music" -> "Plan it" -> "▶ Run it". It appears in Video Studio.

5. THE BUILD CHOICES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOOK (template):
   Clean     - plain and fast.
   News      - crisp colour + sliding "lower-third" bars showing the section label.
   Cinematic - colour-graded + vignette + film grain + black cinema bars (letterbox).
   Bold      - punchy colours.
   All add a smooth animated title, and section titles slide up as they appear.
FORMAT (shape): 16:9 = YouTube · 9:16 = Shorts/Reels/TikTok · 1:1 = square.
RESOLUTION: 1080p (fastest) · 1440p · 4K (sharp, slower) · 8K (very slow, huge).
   4K is the sweet spot. For Shorts pick the 9:16 shape.

8. MUSIC & THE DJ STUDIO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Video Studio -> "Sound Studio (DJ)":
   - Library: generated music moods, sound effects, a bundled pack, your own files,
     and "Find free music online" (legal, no-copyright search).
   - Timeline: place sounds (start time, volume, use only a slice).

   - Videos MADE IN THIS APP: the "🎵 Music" row -> Remove (keeps your voice) or
     Replace with a mood. Replace AUTO-DUCKS: the music dips whenever you speak.

       Online = works out of the box (free, built in), needs internet.
       Local  = offline, best quality (Demucs, installed on this PC).
     Separation is an AI estimate — great for clear speech over music, not perfect.

9. SCENE STUDIO (storyboard + put yourself in a scene)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎬 Scene Studio builds a video one scene at a time:
   - "Plan scenes" turns your script into scene cards (each with an editable prompt).
   - "Generate all" makes an image per scene — watch them appear. ⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume.
   - STORYBOARD: reorder scenes (↑ ↓), remove (✕), or "＋ Add a scene". The order you
     see is the order the video is built in.
   - Rewrite any scene's prompt and "↻ Regenerate this scene".
   - "📎 Put me in (photo)": attach your picture and describe the scene ("me as an
     astronaut, spacesuit, on the moon"). Slider "Photo transform" — lower keeps more
     of your real look. Free; uses internet; the queue can be slow (shows its place).
     Exact face likeness isn't guaranteed on free AI.
   - "🎬 Build video" stitches the approved scenes with narration, your Look, etc.
   - BEAUTIFY: a slider retouches your uploaded photo (skin-smooth + brighten + sharpen)
     on your REAL pixels — no invented face. This is a light retouch, not AI makeup.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9b. STORYBOARD DIRECTOR (direct it shot by shot)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎞 Storyboard Director is for full creative control of the visuals, beat by beat.
   - AUTO mode: paste a whole script (and a title) and the AI DECIDES everything —
     topic, mood, genre, style, and the whole shot breakdown.
   - GUIDED mode: you write the shots yourself, e.g. "0-15s I arrive in a Ferrari and
     say '…'; then 20s a helicopter over the hills, slow fade; then 90s of me speaking".
   Each SHOT has: a length in seconds, a SCENE description (what to show), optional
   narration and an on-screen caption, a camera MOTION, a crossfade INTO it, and its own
   SOUNDS (add a music bed, a sound effect, or your own audio file — each with gain,
   fade-in/out and a start offset). You can reorder shots (↑↓) and delete them (with a
   confirm).
   SUBJECT per shot — who's on screen: (a) Scene only (AI/stock scene), (b) MY PHOTO —
   your real photo is beautified, background-removed and composited INTO the AI scene
   (your true face, not a fake), (c) MY CLIP — your own footage, (d) AI PERSON — a
   generated character (described by you; not your real face).
   BEAUTIFY: "Set my photo", then a slider — RIGHT smooths skin / brightens / evens tone,
   LEFT makes it grittier & sharper. It edits YOUR real pixels (a retouch, not AI makeup).
   Preview it before rendering.
   Build makes each shot, holds it for the right length (narration decides the length, so
   speech is never cut off or overlapped), crossfades between shots, lays narration +
   captions + your sounds, and opens the finished film in the ✂ Timeline Editor so you can
   fine-tune anything.
   HONEST LIMITS (free): AI scenes are STILLS with Ken-Burns motion, not real moving
   footage. Your real face is kept by compositing your ACTUAL photo/clip — the app never
   fabricates a fake video of your face. The background cutout uses a small free model that
   downloads once on first use; if it can't (offline), it falls back to your photo framed
   in the scene, so a render never breaks.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9c. TIMELINE EDITOR (line clips up by hand)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
✂ Timeline Editor is a real non-linear editor. Add video clips (or images) onto a
   track, trim them, reorder them, set a crossfade between clips, add timed text overlays
   (with fades), and place audio on a separate track with gain + fade-in/out — with a
   LIVE WAVEFORM for each audio clip. The mix has a peak limiter so it never clips. It
   renders to one MP4. A Storyboard build opens here automatically (all its shots, sounds
   and captions), so you can add / edit / remove ANY sound or clip after the AI first pass.

9d. LIVE PSX DATA & NCCPL ANALYSIS (real numbers → video)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📈 Live PSX Data: type a PSX symbol (LUCK, HUBC, ENGRO…). The app pulls the stock's
   REAL end-of-day history straight from the PSX data portal and computes — in-app,
   with standard formulas — the latest price + move, 52-week range, 1-year change,
   20/50/200-day moving averages, RSI(14), volume vs average, and a trend read.
   Then: "⬇ Excel" saves an auditable workbook; the "what & how + Language" box +
   "✍ Script" writes a reasoned narration (English / Roman Urdu / Urdu) using ONLY the
   computed numbers (it won't invent figures); "🎬 Build" turns it into a video.
   The maths is exact and tested; the fetch depends on the portal being reachable.

🏦 NCCPL Analysis: NCCPL's portal blocks bots, so this is UPLOAD-based — download the
   FIPI/LIPI (or any market) file from NCCPL yourself and upload it here. The app auto-
   detects and analyses it (foreign vs local net flow, or price/fundamentals), then —
   with the same what/how + Language box — writes a narration script and can build a
   video. All figures come from YOUR file.

Charts tab also does live PSX now: type a symbol → a close-price line + SMA/RSI, or
   import your own OHLC file for full candlesticks; an optional prompt turns it into a
   script too.

12. FAQ
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Do I need to install anything? A: No. Double-click the .exe. (Optional extras:
   the natural voice download and offline Demucs — both already set up here.)
Q: My videos vanished on a new PC. A: You copied only the .exe; copy the WHOLE folder.
Q: The AI says it's busy / fails. A: Free services get busy — try again; the app also
   auto-switches to a backup. Needs internet.
Q: Why is the computer voice robotic? A: That's the Windows voice — use YOUR voice
   (Voice studio) or the Natural voice.
Q: Photo/AI image is slow. A: Free image services queue; it shows your place. It's free.
Q: Can it auto-upload to YouTube? A: It prepares everything and opens your upload page
   so you drop the file — reliable and free. (Full auto-upload would force videos
   private until Google verifies the app.)
Q: Is my stuff private? A: Everything is saved only on your PC. AI features send your
   text/photo to the free online service to do the work. Offline features never leave.
Q: How do I use my own keys? A: Settings has fields (AI Horde for photos, MVSEP for
   online music removal, YouTube channel, paid Claude/OpenAI). All optional.

13. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Nothing on first double-click (portable exe): wait ~60s; don't double-click again.
  (The installed version opens in ~2s — run NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe once to get it.)
- "My updates/changes didn't show up" / old look: check the BUILD BADGE in the sidebar
  (under "OS"). If it's older than expected you launched a STALE copy — this happens when
  you pinned the PORTABLE app to the taskbar/Start while it was running (Windows freezes
  the pin to a temporary unpacked folder). Fix: launch the DESKTOP shortcut or the .exe in
  the folder directly — or switch to the INSTALLED version, which can never go stale this
  way. The exe on disk is always the newest — the pin was pointing at old unpacked code.
- Live PSX Data with no internet / portal down: you'll see an amber banner and your last
  SAVED data (clearly dated). That's deliberate — real numbers from your last fetch, never
  invented ones. Fetch again when you're back online.
- "file not found" after moving: fully close and reopen; keep the whole folder together.
- An online feature errors: check internet, try again.
- A build fails at a weird resolution: it auto-retries safely; if not, use 1080p/4K.
- Writing stopped after changing provider: set it to "Free (online)" in Settings
  (needs no key), or make sure Ollama / your paid key is set up.
- An amber warning appears: "Your ... AI failed — this answer came from the free AI
  instead": your chosen paid/local AI could not answer, so the app used the basic free
  AI (answers will be noticeably weaker). Most common cause: a wrong, expired, or
  out-of-credit API key — open Settings and re-paste your key (an Anthropic key starts
  with "sk-ant-api..." and is very long; copy ALL of it). If your provider is Ollama,
  make sure Ollama is installed and running. The full technical reason is kept in the
  Activity Log. If you see NO warning, your chosen AI answered normally.
- Multiple copies open / won't launch: close all "NIHILPOINTZERO-OS" in Task Manager,
  then open once.

15. PRO TIPS — GET THE BEST RESULTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CONTROL YOUR SCENES/CARDS: put a heading in [SQUARE BRACKETS] on its own line in
  the script (e.g. [THE BIG IDEA]). Each becomes an on-screen section and, in Scene
  Studio, a scene. More brackets = more scene variety, less repetition.
- THE VOICE READS EXACTLY WHAT YOU TYPE: keep sentences short and clean; write
  numbers as words if you want them spoken clearly. Bracketed [HEADINGS] are NOT read
  aloud — they only drive the visuals.
- MOST PRO RESULT: record your OWN voice (🎙 Voice studio) over any build. Use "Redo
  from playhead" to fix one line without redoing everything.
- FOR SHORTS/REELS: Format 9:16 + a Bold or Cinematic Look + "Burn captions" — that
  combo performs best on muted, phone-first feeds.
- MUSIC: prefer "Replace" (it auto-ducks under your voice) over loud background music.
  Keep music as a bed, let the words lead.
- PUT-ME-IN-A-SCENE: lower the "Photo transform" slider to look more like yourself;
  raise it to transform more. Free image queues can be slow — be patient or run big
  jobs (Batch, many scenes) when you're away.
- SPEED: 1080p builds fastest; 4K is the sharp sweet spot; 8K is slow + huge. The free
  AI look uses the internet; the offline "Style presets/Look" builds are fastest.
- OLDER VIDEOS: features like exact music-removal and captions work best on videos
  built AFTER these upgrades — if an old one won't cooperate, rebuild it once.
- MOVE FAST: Ctrl-K jumps to any tab. Your work autosaves ("Saved ✓") and returns
  after a restart, so don't fear closing the app.
- BE ONLINE for the AI parts (writing, images, put-me-in-scene, online music removal);
  everything else works with no internet.
